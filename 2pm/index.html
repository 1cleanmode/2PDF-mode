FAQ

Questions, answered straight

For students, educators, and anyone tired of clunky converters.

Is 2PDFmode really free?

Yes — 100% free, no page limits, no sign-up, no watermarks. It runs entirely in your browser.

How does the rename feature work?

Before you hit Convert, type any filename you want into the "Output filename" field. Whatever you type becomes the PDF's name.

Are my files uploaded anywhere?

No. All processing happens in your browser using JavaScript. Your files never touch a server.

Can I combine images and PDFs into one file?

Yes. Drag in any mix of images and PDFs — they'll be merged in the order you arrange them.

What's the difference between "High" and "Screenshot (PNG)" quality?

"High" compresses to JPEG — smaller files, great for photos. "Screenshot (PNG)" is lossless — bigger files but razor-sharp text.

When are DOCX, XLSX, and Google Drive coming?

They're on the roadmap. Watch this page — when they ship, the 🔒 badges above the tool will disappear.
